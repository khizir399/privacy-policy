DropDeck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

DropDeck (“the extension”) is a Chrome extension published by QuickTech that helps online sellers copy eBay order data, fill delivery addresses on Amazon.de and AliExpress, and check Amazon delivery dates. This policy explains what the extension handles and what it does not.

Summary

DropDeck works entirely inside your browser. It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Information the extension handles

  • eBay order details (order date, order ID, selling price, payout) shown on the eBay order page you are viewing. Read only when you use the Copy feature, and saved in the extension’s on-device clipboard history so you can paste it into your own spreadsheet.
  • Delivery addresses you copy to your clipboard. The extension reads your clipboard only when you click the Autofill button, uses the text to fill the address form on Amazon.de or AliExpress, and briefly keeps it in the browser so it can verify the result on the next Amazon page. It is not saved permanently.
  • Amazon order information (the newest order’s ID, status and estimated delivery date) read from your Amazon “Your Orders” page and stored on your device so the dashboard can show it.
  • Settings and usage counters (format preferences, which tools are switched on, number of copies/fills/checks and a short recent-activity list) stored on your device.

Where it is stored

All of the above is stored using Chrome’s extension storage on your own device. Settings may be synchronized between your own signed-in Chrome browsers by Chrome Sync, which is controlled by Google and your Chrome settings, not by DropDeck. You can delete the stored history at any time from the dashboard, or by removing the extension.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not transmit, sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit or lending decisions, or any purpose unrelated to the features described above.
  • It does not run on websites other than eBay, Amazon and AliExpress pages, and it does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • storage — save your settings, history and counters on your device.
  • clipboardWrite / clipboardRead — copy order rows and dates when you press Copy, and read the address you copied when you press Autofill.
  • tabs — let the dashboard detect whether the current tab is eBay, Amazon or AliExpress and communicate with it.
  • Site access (eBay, Amazon, AliExpress) — add the extension’s buttons to those pages and read the order or address fields you work with.

Children

DropDeck is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the extension’s data handling changes, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date changed.
